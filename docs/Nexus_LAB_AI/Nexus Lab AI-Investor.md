## 1. One-Line Summary

**Nexus Lab AI is an Indian AI systems company building foundational technologies for autonomous agents, persistent memory, AI safety, and sovereign intelligent systems.**

## 2. What We Are Building

Nexus Lab AI is not a single-product startup. It is a research-first company creating the core infrastructure beneath the next generation of AI systems.

Our work focuses on four foundational layers:

- **NALA** — autonomous agent systems
- **AMP** — persistent memory architecture
- **RTA-GUARD** — AI safety and governance
- **Ether** — future distributed intelligence / AI + 6G research

These are not disconnected ideas. They are parts of one mission: building the engineering foundations for trustworthy, sovereign AI.

---

## 3. The Problem

India is adopting AI quickly, but most AI systems are still built on foreign infrastructure and assumptions.

That creates three structural gaps:

### Data sovereignty
Sensitive enterprise, government, and strategic workloads need AI that can operate without exposing data to external cloud dependence.

### Reliability
Current AI systems can be powerful, but they remain fragile in long-running workflows, recovery scenarios, and governed execution environments.

### India-specific intelligence
India needs AI that can operate across local contexts, multilingual environments, and sovereign deployment requirements.

The missing layer is not just more applications. It is original AI systems infrastructure.

## 4. Our Solution

Nexus Lab AI is building the systems layer required for sovereign intelligent systems.

### NALA
An autonomous agent framework for planning, execution, recovery, and multi-agent coordination.

### AMP
A memory architecture designed for persistent identity, state continuity, and long-term recall.

### RTA-GUARD
A deterministic safety and governance layer for policy enforcement, secure execution, and reduced AI failure modes.

### Ether
A future-facing research track exploring how AI can operate across next-generation communication and edge infrastructure.
