## 1. Overview & Learning Progression Philosophy

To master the theoretical and systems foundations of NALA, a learner must follow a **"Roots Before Fruits"** trajectory. Jumping directly into complex agent failure analysis without understanding vector norms, POSIX file semantics, or graph acyclicity leads to superficial memorization.

